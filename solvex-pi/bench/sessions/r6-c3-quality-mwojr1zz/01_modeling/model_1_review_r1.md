## 3. Issues Found:

1. **F5 caption r contradiction (blocking — specific wrong number).** The report and `results_summary.md` attach **r = 0.70** to `figures/F5_phi_vs_judge_z.png` ("recovered φ̂ vs. within-week judge z"), and separately quote "correlation of recovered shares with judge shares r = 0.697." But `results.json → figure_stats.r_phi_vs_judgez = 0.3854`. φ̂-vs-judge-z and φ̂-vs-judge-share are different statistics; 0.70/0.697 cannot both be the r of the judge-z figure whose stored stat is 0.385. Either the caption copies the judge-share correlation onto a judge-z plot, or the stored figure stat belongs to a different pairing. As written, the figure claim is contradicted by the model's own results file.
2. **Judge-only baseline not traceable and apparently inconsistent (blocking — specific suspect numbers).** The report claims "judge-only argmin hit rate on exact-regime singles is **40.9%**," which appears nowhere in `results.json`; the only related stored metric is `hit_rate_single_judge_only = 0.1499`. If 0.1499 is a hard argmin hit rate over single-exit weeks (233) or all exit weeks (265), then by subset inclusion the exact-regime (186) rate cannot reach 40.9% (≤ 34.9–39.7 hits in the superset vs. ≈ 76 needed at 40.9%). Both numbers can only be simultaneously true if 0.1499 is actually a *mean judge capture probability* (cf. `mean_p_capture_judge_exact = 0.134`) — in which case the key name is wrong and the hard 40.9% must itself be stored. Either way one of the two numbers is mislabeled, and a headline baseline comparison rests on an undefined metric.
3. **Quoted LOO exit-week τ = 0.712 not stored (minor — traceability).** `results.json` carries `loo_tau_mean`, `loo_tau_season_min`, `loo_tau_min` but not the exit-week-scope mean 0.712. It is presumably derivable from `model1_loo.csv`, but headline quoted numbers must live in `results.json`.
4. **Wrong explanation for ε_s ≈ 0 (minor — documentation).** All 34 stored season effects are ~1e-16. A N(0, 0.75²) prior cannot pin 34 MAP values to zero to machine precision; the accurate statement is that the season terms never moved off their zero initialization (per-run popularity g1 absorbs season-level signal). The current wording ("within identifiability tolerance given the soft season priors") is mathematically incorrect and matters for how Models 2/3 use season effects.
5. **Bookkeeping nits (minor).** Composition of `n_params = 3654` (2,777 v + per-run g1/ka + season ε) is undocumented and does not decompose under the obvious reading; `map_theta_sd_v = 0.0435` is a posterior sd, not a fitted population sd, and sits unlabeled beside `g1_sd`/`ka_sd`; typos ("fan/latid space", stray "fromPublic") should be cleaned before the paper phase.

**What is *not* in question:** the market-share/Plackett–Luce mathematics, the elimination-constraint construction, the deliverable φ̂ surface and draws, and every gate number listed in §1. The blocking issues are statistics/labeling defects in the report layer, not in the model; no fan-share, exit-fit, or draw deliverable needs to change.
