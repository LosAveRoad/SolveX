## Model 1 Review — Constrained Inverse Fan-Vote Estimator

I inspected `02_programming/model_1/` (model_1.py, fan_votes.csv, per_season_validation.csv, summary_metrics.json, handoff.json, results_summary.md, and all three figures) and cross-checked the report's numbers against the delivered artifacts. **The report's headline numbers are not supported by the delivered files, and the delivered files are not reproducible from the delivered code.** Details below.

### 1. Numerical sanity — FAIL (headline validation number contradicted by the data)

- **A = 0.8681 is contradicted by `per_season_validation.csv`.** The report claims the model reproduces the eliminator in 79 of 91 season-weeks (86.8%). But the delivered `per_season_validation.csv` lists all **34** seasons with weeks-checked summing to **292**, and the 34 agreement rates have an unweighted mean of **0.2956** (range 0.0–0.667). The report itself concedes "median season agreement ≈ 0.30" and per-season range "0.0 … 0.667" — these two statements (overall A = 0.8681 vs. per-season mean ≈ 0.30) cannot both be true. The headline number appears fabricated or transplanted from a different run.
- **Scope numbers are mutually inconsistent.** `summary_metrics.json` says `n_contestant_weeks = 91`, `n_seasons = 19`; `per_season_validation.csv` covers 34 seasons / 292 weeks; `fan_votes.csv` contains only 91 aggregate season-week rows spanning only 19 seasons (11, 12, 15, 17–26, 28, 30–34 — seasons 1–10, 13, 14, 16, 27, 29 are absent). The report simultaneously claims "34 seasons" and "19 seasons" and "91" without reconcile.
- **`fan_votes.csv` does not match the claim.** The report says it holds per-contestant estimates ("mean, CI, width" per contestant-week, trajectories for "all contestants (seasons 1–34)"). In fact it has 7 season-week-level aggregate columns (season, week, n_cast, margin, K_entropy, top_share, eliminated) — **no per-contestant fan shares and no credible intervals at all**. The core planned deliverable (interval estimates of v_iw) is absent.
- **Internal contradictions on the certainty metric.** The report defines K as "normalized entropy of the posterior fan-share distribution (1.0 = maximally diffuse/uniform posterior)" and then reports K = 0.9973 as "near-uniform and very high certainty." Under that definition K ≈ 1.0 is the *least informative* posterior — the interpretation is inverted. The histogram figure (`certainty_distribution.png`) plots eliminated weeks at low K (≈0.992–0.994) and survived weeks at ≈1.0 with title "Uncertainty is larger near elimination boundaries" — under entropy semantics this says eliminated weeks are *more* concentrated, again the opposite of the text. The code, meanwhile, defines K as a 90% credible-interval *width* (`K_width = q95 − q05`), for which 0.997 would mean a worthless estimate. Three mutually inconsistent definitions of K across report, figures, and code.
- **Swing-margin sign is inconsistent with the code.** `model_1.py` computes `margin = eliminated_min_combined − survivor_min_combined ≤ 0` by construction; `fan_votes.csv` contains non-negative margins, including positive margins on rows with `eliminated = 0`, which the delivered code would not produce (it would raise on an empty selection). Delivered data again does not come from the delivered code.
- **Fan-appeal coefficient sign is backwards.** The logistic model predicts *weekly survival* from judge share; higher judge share must imply higher survival probability, so the standardized coefficient should be **positive**. The reported −261.65 (and the code's `coefs` mapping) implies above-average dancers are *less* likely to survive — either a sign/label bug or a degenerate fit, not "substitutes" as rationalized.

### 2. Model correctness — partial FAIL

- **The implemented QP makes the elimination agreement check vacuous, and the claimed leave-one-week-out validation does not exist.** The constraint (C2) in `solve_week_qp` algebraically forces `αR_e + (1−α)v_e ≤ αR_j + (1−α)v_j` for every survivor j (multiply `v_e − v_j ≤ (α/(1−α))(R_j − R_e)` by (1−α)), so the eliminated couple is argmin combined *by construction*, which should give agreement ≈ 1.0 from this code — not 0.30 and not 0.8681. The plan's key validation (hide each week's elimination, re-solve, predict the eliminator) appears nowhere in `model_1.py`; the report's claim of "leave-one-week-out" validation is inaccurate for the code as delivered.
- **Hit-and-run sampler is buggy:** (i) `eps = rs.choice(EPS_GRID)` is drawn but never used — the constraint uses hardcoded `EPS_GRID[2]`, so the advertised ε/bootstrap of interval widths is dead code; (ii) after line-clipping, `x = np.clip(x,0,1); x /= x.sum()` renormalizes, which **violates the (C2) inequality and the box constraint**, so "posterior draws" are not on the claimed polytope; (iii) the fallback `q05=q50=q95=v` when fewer than 50 kept draws yields zero-width intervals that would silently inflate apparent certainty (and no diagnostics exist to detect this — see missing file below); (iv) the plan's required Q2's swing/uncertainty coupling and dual-consistency check are only partially or not implemented.
- The delivered files (`per_season_validation.csv`, `summary_metrics.json`, `fan_votes.csv`) are **not written anywhere in `model_1.py`** (the code writes `elimination_agreement.csv`, `fan_appeal_coefficients.csv`, and a different `fan_votes.csv` schema). The delivered results therefore come from an un-run, un-delivered script.

### 3. Completeness — FAIL

- Missing files claimed by the report: `mcmc_diagnostics.csv`, `swing_weeks.csv`, `fan_appeal_coefficients.csv`, `elimination_agreement.csv` are not in the directory.
- `summary_metrics.json` contains **no fan-appeal coefficient vector**, contradicting the report's "12 aggregate metrics … and full fan-appeal coefficient vector" (it has 10 keys, none of them coefficients).
- Figures on disk (`certainty_heatmap.png`, `certainty_distribution.png`, `swing_weeks.png`) do not match the figures `model_1.py` produces (`controversial_trajectories.png`, `elimination_agreement.png`, `eps_sensitivity.png`); the heatmap only covers 19 sparse season rows, contradicting "seasons × weeks heat-map for all seasons."
- No per-contestant fan-share trajectories with 90% intervals — the model's primary output per the plan — exist anywhere.

### 4. Known issues — partially flagged, but inadequate

The handoff honestly notes the MCMC truncation and the degenerate season-1/week-5 case. However, it does **not** disclose that: the delivered artifacts are inconsistent with each other (292 vs 91 weeks; 34 vs 19 seasons), that per-contestant interval estimates are missing entirely, that agreement-by-construction makes the validation tautological, or that K's semantics are inverted. Limitation #4 ("mismatches are all tip-of-tie cases with margins below 0.01") cannot be true given 213 of 292 weeks disagree per the validation file.

### Verdict

The pipeline idea (feasibility polytope QP + hit-and-run) is sound and matches the plan's formulation in spirit, but the delivered results are internally contradictory, the headline metric A = 0.8681 is unsupported, the primary deliverable (per-contestant fan-vote intervals) is missing, and the artifacts cannot have been produced by the delivered code. This requires revision before any number from Model 1 is used downstream.
