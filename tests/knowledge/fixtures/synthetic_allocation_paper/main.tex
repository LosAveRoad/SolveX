\begin{abstract}
This synthetic fixture allocates limited patrol teams across wildlife reserve
zones.  It is solely an automated-test corpus, not a competition submission.
\end{abstract}

\section{Problem Context}
Reserve managers must assign patrol teams to zones with different risk scores.
The allocation should cover high-risk zones while respecting the available
staffing budget.

\input{sections/method}

\section{Data Inputs}
The fixture uses synthetic risk scores, staffing efforts, and a single patrol
budget.  No external dataset or published result is represented here.

\section{Baseline Policy}
A simple baseline assigns teams in descending zone order without considering
the joint staffing limit across the chosen zones.

\section{Sensitivity Analysis}
The test corpus describes a hypothetical change in the budget and compares the
chosen zones without reporting any competition result.

\section{Limitations}
This miniature example omits travel time, uncertainty, and operational details
so that it remains a compact automated-test fixture.

\section{Conclusion}
The integer programming formulation makes the resource allocation decision
auditable for each reserve zone.
