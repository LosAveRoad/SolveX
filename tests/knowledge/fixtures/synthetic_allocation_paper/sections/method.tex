\section{Integer Programming Formulation}
We use a binary integer programming model for patrol resource allocation.
Let $x_i$ equal one when a patrol team is assigned to reserve zone $i$.
Each selected zone contributes its estimated conservation risk score $r_i$.

\begin{equation}
\max \sum_i r_i x_i
\end{equation}

subject to the staffing constraint
\begin{equation}
\sum_i c_i x_i \leq B,
\end{equation}
where $c_i$ is the patrol effort and $B$ is the total available budget.
This integer programming formulation prioritizes high-risk zones while
preserving a transparent resource allocation policy.
